## What happened

The prover sat warm overnight. The morning dough overproofed and four trays were waste.

## How it was found

The baker logged the temperature at the start of the mix and stopped.

## Impact

Four trays of waste, weighed and written down.

## What changed

The prover temperature is logged at the start of the mix. See RULE-23.
