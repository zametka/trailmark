## What happened

A cream cake for a 15:00 window left in a paper bag. It slumped.

## How it was found

The customer sent a photograph the same afternoon.

## Impact

One cake replaced. No other order that day was cold.

## What changed

Cold cakes leave only in a chilled bag. See RULE-6.
